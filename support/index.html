Contact support

What to include

  • Product name and version.
  • Operating system or device, where relevant.
  • A clear description of the issue.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue, if possible.
  • Relevant error messages or screenshots.

Do not send passwords, license secrets, medical records, or other sensitive information through an ordinary support form.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

DropSpher, DigiSpherEMR, and LandlordPro keep their records on the computer or phone. Business QR keeps its library on the phone and also shows Google ads. InvoSpher keeps invoices and client records in an account. Check the product page before you rely on it.

How do I move a product to a new device?

Contact support before removing the app or resetting the old device. Some products have export or backup features; others need a guided transfer so records are not lost.

How do I get an installer or activation help?

Email support with the product name and the email address used for the purchase. Do not send license secrets or passwords in the first message.

Can I use one license on several devices?

License terms differ by product. DigiSpherEMR is one Windows computer, or two computers in the same clinic when the license includes inventory. Ask before installing any product on an additional device.

Does this website take payments?

No. This website does not process orders or payments. Purchases happen through the channel named on the product page, such as Google Play, and that channel's terms apply.

How quickly will I hear back?

Support is handled by the team that builds the products. Response time depends on the request and the current workload. Include the details above so the first reply can be useful.
