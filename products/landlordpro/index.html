LandlordPro is for a small landlord who checks a notebook, a chat, or a spreadsheet to see what came in. The property, the tenant, and the payment sit in one record on the phone.

The awkward moment is the end of the month: who paid, who is late, and whether a repair ate the margin. That answer is usually split across messages and a list you do not quite trust.

The dashboard opens on that month. Expected income, collected rent, and the outstanding amount sit together. Occupancy sits beside net income. An overdue payment is called out, and a chart sets collected rent against expenses. The rest of the book is Tenants, Finances, Properties, and Settings. A profile can carry a name and a company for the receipt. The currency is chosen once and used everywhere. USD is the starting choice. The published name on Google Play is LandlordPro - Rent Tracker.

LandlordPro keeps the landlord's own books. It does not collect the rent, and it does not replace a lease.

Where the rent book breaks

Portfolio

Each property lives in a different note

Give a property a name and an address, and the portfolio shows units, how many are occupied, estimated income, and the next due date. The free plan stops at 3 properties.

Lease

The terms are a photo of the contract

A tenant has a name, a phone number, a unit, and a rent amount. Rent can be monthly, weekly, or daily, with a due date and lease dates. An optional security deposit is marked held. Status includes paid for the current cycle and moved out. The list can be filtered and searched. The free plan stops at 3 tenants.

Twice

The same payment gets written down twice

Record a payment as cash, bank transfer, mobile money, card, or other. Once a tenant is paid for the current cycle, that tenant leaves the collect list until the next due date. The app can prepare a text receipt with the amount, method, period, and any remaining balance, then hand it to WhatsApp, SMS, or the phone's share sheet.

Chase

The reminder is rewritten every time

From a tenant, the app fills a reminder with the name, the property, the amount, and the due date. You send it by WhatsApp or SMS. LandlordPro does not send those messages on its own.

Repairs

The month looks better because the repair was forgotten

Pro can log maintenance, repairs, utilities, taxes, insurance, mortgage, or another cost, against a property or left unassigned. Those amounts feed the dashboard margin: collected rent minus expenses. Expense tracking is not included on the free plan.

Copy

The phone is the only copy of the book

Pro can keep an automatic local snapshot every day or every week. A manual backup opens the share sheet so you can put the database file where you choose. Restore reads a database file back in. DigiSpher does not host that copy.

A small book, then a longer one

Free

Up to 3 tenants and 3 properties. Dashboard, payments, receipts, and reminders. Expenses and automatic backup are not included.

Pro

Unlimited tenants and properties, expense tracking, and backup. A purchase can be restored from the app.

Privacy

The book is only useful if it stays yours

Tenants, properties, payments, expenses, and settings are stored in a SQLite database on the phone. The privacy screen in the app, dated August 20, 2026, says those records are not uploaded and that the app does not collect analytics. Google sign-in is optional and is used for the landlord profile. Subscription checks go through Google Play. WhatsApp or SMS opens only when you choose to send a reminder, a receipt, or a support message.

Do not send tenant details through the DigiSpher contact form.
