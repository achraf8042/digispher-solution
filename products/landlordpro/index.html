Overview What it keeps Plans Privacy

The dashboard opens on the month: expected income, how much is already collected, and what is still outstanding. Occupancy sits beside net income. An overdue payment is called out, and a chart sets collected rent against expenses.

Five tabs cover the work: Dashboard, Tenants, Finances, Properties, and Settings. A landlord profile can carry a name and a company, and those details can appear on a receipt. The currency is chosen once and used everywhere. USD is the starting choice.

LandlordPro keeps the landlord's own books. It does not collect the rent, and it does not replace a lease.

What the landlord keeps

01

Properties

Each property has a name and an address. The portfolio shows units, how many are occupied, estimated income, and the next due date. The free plan stops at 3 properties.

02

Tenants and the lease

A tenant has a name, a phone number, a unit, and a rent amount. Rent can be monthly, weekly, or daily, with a due date and lease dates. An optional security deposit is marked held. Status includes paid for the current cycle and moved out. The list can be filtered and searched. The free plan stops at 3 tenants.

03

Payments and receipts

Record a payment as cash, bank transfer, mobile money, card, or other. Once a tenant is paid for the current cycle, that tenant leaves the collect list until the next due date, so the same cycle is not entered twice. The app can prepare a text receipt with the amount, method, period, and any remaining balance, then hand it to WhatsApp, SMS, or the phone's share sheet.

04

Reminders you send

From a tenant, the app fills a reminder with the name, the property, the amount, and the due date. You send it by WhatsApp or SMS. LandlordPro does not send those messages on its own.

05

Expenses

Pro can log maintenance, repairs, utilities, taxes, insurance, mortgage, or another cost, against a property or left unassigned. Those amounts feed the dashboard margin: collected rent minus expenses. Expense tracking is not included on the free plan.

06

A backup on the device

Pro can keep an automatic local snapshot every day or every week. A manual backup opens the share sheet so you can put the database file where you choose. Restore reads a database file back in. DigiSpher does not host that copy.

Free, then Pro

Free

$0

Up to 3 tenants and 3 properties. Dashboard, payments, receipts, and reminders. Expenses and automatic backup are not included.

Pro

Unlimited tenants and properties, expense tracking, and backup. Google Play shows the current price for a monthly plan, a yearly plan, or a one-time lifetime purchase. A purchase can be restored from the app.

Privacy

The rent book is not uploaded as part of ordinary use

Tenants, properties, payments, expenses, and settings are stored in a SQLite database on the phone. The privacy screen in the app, dated August 20, 2026, says those records are not uploaded and that the app does not collect analytics. Google sign-in is optional and is used for the landlord profile. Subscription checks go through Google Play. WhatsApp or SMS opens only when you choose to send a reminder, a receipt, or a support message.

Do not send tenant details through the DigiSpher contact form.
