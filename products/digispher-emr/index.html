Overview What it covers License

Open the day on a dashboard that shows active patients, today's consultations, collected revenue, and the visits already on the book. From the same screen you can register a patient, book a visit, write a note, write a prescription, or issue an invoice.

DigiSpherEMR is practice-management software. It keeps the record and the paperwork together. It does not replace a clinician's judgment, and this website does not book patient visits. The product site is published in English and French.

What the application covers

01

Dashboard

Active patients, consultations documented today, revenue collected today, visits scheduled today, and consultations for the week. A clinical feed lists recent notes and prescriptions. A financial feed lists recent invoices as paid, unpaid, or partial. Shortcuts along the dock start the next task.

02

Patient chart

One record holds identity, date of birth, blood group, contact details, and an emergency contact. Allergies carry a severity. Vitals can include blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, weight, height, a calculated BMI, oxygen saturation, and respiratory rate. Past notes, prescriptions, appointments, and invoices stay on that same chart.

03

SOAP notes

A consultation is written as subjective, objective, assessment, and plan. Templates cover a general consult, an annual physical, a follow-up, and a standard SOAP note. A note can stay in draft and then be finalized with a timestamp. Search covers patient name, date, and diagnosis words.

04

Prescriptions

A prescription can list several medicines, each with name, dose, how often it is taken, and how long it continues. Known allergies for that patient are shown while the prescription is written. The result is an A5 PDF with the clinic header, the doctor's details, a medicine table, patient instructions, and a signature block. Print it or save the PDF.

05

Appointments

Each visit stores the patient, date and time, doctor, and reason. Status moves through scheduled, confirmed, in progress, completed, cancelled, and no-show. The list can be filtered to today, this week, or the month, with a calendar for choosing the day.

06

Billing and the practice books

Invoices itemize consultations, procedures, and tests, with a subtotal and a tax rate you set. Mark each invoice unpaid, partial, or paid, and give the patient a PDF receipt. Expenses can be logged under rent, supplies, utilities, software, equipment, staff, or other. The finance view shows today's revenue, the month's revenue and expenses, net profit, and amounts still outstanding, plus a daily comparison of money in and money out. Invoices and expenses export to CSV for an accountant.

07

Two computers, one clinic

The two-computer license is for one practice, typically a doctor computer and a reception computer on the same private network. Inventory, batches, and stock movements are included only on that license. The one-computer license does not include inventory.

08

Privacy on the desk

Privacy mode shortens surnames on the dashboard and lists, for example when the screen is shared. The session can lock itself after a chosen idle time. Closing the application writes a timestamped backup and keeps a rolling set of recent copies on the computer. The database is not uploaded as part of ordinary use.
