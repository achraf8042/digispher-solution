The job is usually small and urgent. The contract has to be editable, the attachment has to fit in an email, or the photo should not still say where it was taken. A website that accepts the upload solves the format and creates a second problem.

The home screen is a drop area and a count of work already done on that machine: operations finished, QR codes generated, and space saved. From there the app opens the suite that matches the file: Images, PDFs, Videos, Audio, or Developer tools. Several files can wait in a queue. Each finished job shows the size before and after. A history of completed jobs stays in a local database on the computer. The window is labeled DropZone. The product name is DropSpher.

The PDF will not do the next job

  • You need to edit the words. A text-based PDF can be rebuilt as a .docx, including tables, columns, headers, and footers.
  • You need pages you can place in a message. Render a page range to PNG or JPEG at 150, 200, or 300 DPI.
  • The file is too large to send. Compress by optimizing streams and fonts, without turning the page into a blurry picture.
  • You have the wrong pages. Merge files in an order you set, split a document, pull out a range, or rotate pages by 90, 180, or 270 degrees.
  • You need what is inside, not the PDF. Extract embedded images, or pull the text out as plain text.

The picture is the wrong file

  • The next program rejects the format. Convert among PNG, JPEG, WEBP, BMP, TIFF, GIF, and ICO.
  • Several pictures should be one document. Combine them into a PDF.
  • You need an icon, not a photo. Build a Windows .ico at 16, 32, 48, 64, 128, and 256 pixels, or a web favicon pack with a site manifest.
  • The image is heavier than the page that will hold it. Scale by percent or by pixel size, and compress with a quality slider while keeping transparency.
  • The photo still says where it was taken. Remove EXIF details such as GPS coordinates, camera identity, and timestamps.

The clip is too big, or you only need the sound

  • The player or the site wants a different format. Convert among MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, WEBM, and GIF, using FFmpeg included with the app.
  • The upload limit is the real deadline. Compress with a CRF quality setting and optionally scale to 1080p, 720p, or 480p.
  • A short loop is enough. Turn a clip into a GIF at 10, 15, 20, 24, or 30 frames a second.
  • The picture is in the way of the audio. Save the track as MP3, WAV, AAC, FLAC, or OGG.
  • The audio file is the wrong format too. Convert among MP3, AAC, WAV, FLAC, OGG, and M4A, from 64 kbps up to 320 kbps.
