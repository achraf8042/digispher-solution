Convert the file without uploading it.

DropSpher is a Windows desktop app for PDFs, images, video, audio, and data files. You drop a file in, choose the job, and the work runs on that computer.

The home screen is a drop area and a count of work already done on that machine: operations finished, QR codes generated, and space saved. From there the app opens Images, PDFs, Videos, Audio, or Developer tools. The window is labeled DropZone. The product name is DropSpher.

Several files can wait in a queue. Each finished job shows the size before and after. If a job fails or you cancel it, the original file is left as it was. A history of completed jobs stays in a local database on the computer.

PDF

  • Word. A text-based PDF can be rebuilt as a .docx, including tables, columns, headers, and footers.
  • Images. Render pages to PNG or JPEG at 150, 200, or 300 DPI, for a page range you choose.
  • Size. Compress by optimizing streams and fonts, without turning the page into a blurry picture.
  • Pages. Merge files in an order you set, split a document, pull out a range, or rotate pages by 90, 180, or 270 degrees.
  • Contents. Extract embedded images, or pull the text out as plain text.

Images

  • Formats. PNG, JPEG, WEBP, BMP, TIFF, GIF, and ICO.
  • PDF. Combine several pictures into one PDF.
  • Icons. Build a Windows .ico at 16, 32, 48, 64, 128, and 256 pixels, or a web favicon pack with a site manifest.
  • Size and quality. Scale by percent or by pixel size, and compress with a quality slider while keeping transparency.
  • Metadata. Remove EXIF details such as GPS coordinates, camera identity, and timestamps.

Video and audio

  • Video formats. MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, WEBM, and GIF, using FFmpeg included with the app.
  • Smaller video. Compress with a CRF quality setting and optionally scale to 1080p, 720p, or 480p.
  • GIF. Turn a clip into a looping GIF at 10, 15, 20, 24, or 30 frames a second.
  • Audio from video. Save the track as MP3, WAV, AAC, FLAC, or OGG.
  • Audio files. Convert among MP3, AAC, WAV, FLAC, OGG, and M4A, from 64 kbps up to 320 kbps.

Data and developer files

CSV and Excel

CSV becomes a styled .xlsx, with encoding and delimiter detected and a frozen header row. A workbook can go back to CSV, including a zip of every sheet.

Hashes and Base64

MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, and SHA-512, copied from the app. Encode or decode Base64, including URL-safe text and a data URI.

JSON and QR

Format, check, or minify JSON. Generate a QR code on the computer for a URL, Wi-Fi details, or plain text, with a title on the image.

The DropSpher site describes the early-access purchase as a lifetime license for two personal computers, with a year of major feature updates and continued bug fixes. Windows is the system it ships for. macOS and Linux are noted as prepared.
