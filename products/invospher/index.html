Overview Where invoices get stuck Plans Privacy

A finished job often leaves three scraps: a client name in a chat, a total in a note, and a question about whether the money arrived. Adding those up again is the evening InvoSpher is meant to shorten.

The home screen is that reading. Total revenue sits at the top in the currency you use. Pending and overdue amounts sit beside it. Recent activity shows the invoice number, the client, the amount, and a status such as paid. Under that, two actions start the work: a new invoice, or a new client. The bottom of the app is four places: Home, Clients, Invoices, and Settings.

A free account can be created in the app. Invoices and client records stay with that account. Premium adds a logo, a signature, company colors, the fuller client record, and payment reminders. The paid plan can be cancelled from the app settings. The app lists support for a large set of currencies. Tax on an invoice comes from a rate you set.

Privacy

Invoices stay with the account

InvoSpher is the DigiSpher product that uses an account. Invoices, client records, and the business details you add are stored so you can open them again from the app. The InvoSpher privacy policy says you can export that data, and that it is removed from InvoSpher servers after the account is closed.

Do not send client lists or invoice details through the DigiSpher contact form.
