Overview How an invoice gets made Plans

The home screen is a short reading of the business. Total revenue sits at the top in the currency you use. Pending and overdue amounts sit beside it. Under that, two actions start the work: new invoice, or new client.

Recent activity lists invoice numbers, the client name, the amount, and a status such as paid. The bottom of the app is four places: Home, Clients, Invoices, and Settings.

A free account can be created from the app. Paid plans add a logo, a signature, company colors, the fuller client record, and payment reminders. Cancel the paid plan from the app settings. The InvoSpher page also lists a 14-day trial on Premium and support for a large set of currencies.
