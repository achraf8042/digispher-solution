Overview How an invoice gets made Plans Privacy

The home screen is a short reading of the business. Total revenue sits at the top in the currency you use. Pending and overdue amounts sit beside it. Under that, two actions start the work: new invoice, or new client.

Recent activity lists invoice numbers, the client name, the amount, and a status such as paid. The bottom of the app is four places: Home, Clients, Invoices, and Settings.

A free account can be created from the app, and invoices and client records are kept with that account. Premium adds a logo, a signature, company colors, the fuller client record, and payment reminders. The paid plan can be cancelled from the app settings. The app also lists support for a large set of currencies.

Annual

Privacy

Invoices stay with the account

InvoSpher is the DigiSpher product that uses an account. Invoices, client records, and the business details you add are stored so you can open them again from the app. The InvoSpher privacy policy says you can export that data, and that it is removed from InvoSpher servers after the account is closed.

Do not send client lists or invoice details through the DigiSpher contact form.
