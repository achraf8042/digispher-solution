Cookie Policy

The site does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Here is what it does load.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a site can store on your device. Similar technologies include local storage and session storage. This page describes what this website actually uses.

2. Cookies this site sets

None. No analytics, advertising, or preference cookies are set by DigiSpher's own code. The contact form uses session storage for a short rate limit. That value is not a cookie, stays on your device, and is cleared when the tab closes.

3. Third-party requests

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and layout and icon files from jsDelivr. Those requests share your IP address and browser details with the providers. They may set their own cookies under their own policies. DigiSpher does not control those cookies.

4. Why there is no consent banner

The site does not run optional analytics or advertising cookies, so there is nothing optional to accept. If that changes, a choice will be offered before those tools run where the law requires it.

5. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking third-party requests may affect fonts and icons, but the content of the site will still be readable.

6. Changes

If the cookie setup changes, this page will be updated to match the live site.
